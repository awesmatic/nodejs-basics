About

A little about how I work.

I'm a frontend developer who can hold their own on the backend.

My core is React, TypeScript, and Tailwind CSS — that's where I do my best work. I've built admin panels, marketing sites, and reusable component libraries, wired up REST APIs with React Query, and managed state with Redux Toolkit and Recoil. Next.js is my go-to when a project needs server rendering or file-based routing.

What makes me useful on a full-stack team is that I don't stop at the API boundary. I've shipped backend features with Node.js, Express, and PostgreSQL — writing endpoints, debugging queries, and understanding what happens when a request goes out. That means I can pick up a ticket on either side of the stack and not just throw it back over the fence.

I enjoy the middle of a project most — when the design is settled and the real engineering starts. Breaking screens into reusable components, handling the loading and error states nobody designed, tightening performance, and making the whole thing feel instant.

I took a planned career break in early 2024 for personal reasons. I used the time to go deeper on TypeScript and Next.js and to build the projects above from scratch. I'm now looking for a frontend or full-stack role where I can contribute from week one.
